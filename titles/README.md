# Titles

One record per game (`*.json`): its title, its English name, the IDs of its releases and its
entries on other sites. `names/` has the names of each game's people, places and things, with
their readings, from which the names dictionaries (`names-*.zip`) are made.

The names come from [VNDB](https://vndb.org)'s character data, except where a file's `source`
says `by_hand`. This folder's data is under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0, and its
contents under the Database Contents License (DbCL) v1.0: see [LICENSE](LICENSE). If you use or
share it, credit VNDB and keep it under the same terms.
